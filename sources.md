## 03 — EU and Indonesia sign trade deal after 10 years
- Reuters, "EU and Indonesia sign trade deal after 10 years of
  negotiations", 21 Nov 2026
  https://www.reuters.com/markets/commodities/eu-indonesia-sign-trade-deal-after-10-years-negotiations-2026-11-21/
  (The EU and Indonesia signed a comprehensive trade agreement and an
  investment protection agreement on Saturday; duties removed on more
  than 90% of products; the deal cuts EU exporters' duty bill by
  €650 million; likely in effect in early 2027 after European
  Parliament approval.)
- Council of the European Union, "EU-Indonesia: Council greenlights
  signature of landmark trade and investment agreements", 18 Nov 2026
  https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/11/18/eu-indonesia-council-greenlights-signature-of-landmark-trade-and-investment-agreements/
  (Once fully implemented, up to 98.5% of tariff lines liberalised;
  Indonesian import duties on EU cars, up to 50% today, eliminated
  within five years; savings of around €600 million a year in customs
  duties.)
- Figure note: the European Commission's estimate is ~€600 million a
  year; Reuters reports a €650 million duty-bill cut. The image uses
  the Commission's "about €600 million".
